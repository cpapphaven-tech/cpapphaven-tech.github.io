Frequently Asked Questions — 2 Player Games

How do two players play on a single keyboard or screen?

Turn-based games (like Chess, Checkers, and Four Colors) allow players to take alternate turns, while real-time sports (like Air Hockey) feature split-touch or dual-key controls.

Do 2 player games require two controllers?

No controllers needed! All titles are mapped for touchscreen taps or standard keyboard inputs (such as WASD and Arrow Keys).

Can I also play these games solo against the computer?

Yes! Every 2 player game on PlayMix includes built-in AI bot opponents, so you can still hone your skills solo whenever a friend isn't nearby.
