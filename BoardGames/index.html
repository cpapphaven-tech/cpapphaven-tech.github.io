Classic Board Games Reimagined for the Modern Browser

Board games have been the centrepiece of human social interaction for thousands of years — from ancient Egyptian Senet to Chess in the Persian courts to the global Ludo phenomenon. What makes board games uniquely enduring is their perfect balance of strategy, social interaction, luck, and skill. In the digital age, PlayMix has brought this timeless tradition online — without losing any of the charm. Our Chess Master features multiple AI difficulty levels, from beginner to advanced, with a beautifully rendered 3D board that makes each match feel ceremonial. Ludo Classic faithfully recreates the beloved colour-and-counter formula with animated dice rolls and smooth piece movement. Snakes & Ladders introduces genuine suspense with every dice throw. Backgammon Classic brings the ancient strategy game to mobile with intuitive drag-and-drop piece control. Connect Four delivers instant competitive depth — easy to learn in 30 seconds, requiring deep strategy to master. What unites all PlayMix board games is our commitment to both solo and social play: every game has a competent AI so you always have an opponent, and every game supports local 2-player mode so friends and family can compete face-to-face. No physical board needed — your screen is the game board, and the fun is just a click away.
