🎮 How to Play Checkers

  1. Piece Placement: Played on an 8x8 checkerboard with 12 pieces per side positioned on the dark squares.
  2. Moving: Regular pieces move forward diagonally by one unoccupied square.
  3. Jumping & Capturing: If an opponent's piece is diagonally adjacent and the square behind it is empty, you must jump over and capture it! If another jump is available from that landing square, you must continue the multi-jump chain.
  4. Crowning Kings: When your piece reaches the farthest back row, it is crowned a 👑 King and can move both forwards and backwards.
  5. Winning: You win by capturing all opposing pieces or by trapping your opponent so they have no legal moves remaining.

🧠 Master Strategies for Checkers

  • Protect the Back Row: Keep your back-row anchor pieces (Row 7 for Red) in place as long as possible to prevent your opponent from crowning early Kings.
  • Dominate the Center: Center squares (D4, E4, D5, E5) provide maximum diagonal attack angles. Side edges restrict your mobility.
  • Sacrifice for King Advantage: Giving up a normal piece to gain a King is often a game-winning trade.
