🎮 How to Play Connect Four

  1. Choose a Column: The board consists of a vertical grid with 7 columns and 6 rows (42 slots). On your turn, tap or click the column where you want to drop your colored disc.
  2. Gravity Mechanics: The disc drops to the lowest unoccupied slot in the chosen column.
  3. Take Turns: Red and Yellow alternate turns dropping one disc at a time.
  4. Win Condition: The first player to form a straight line of 4 discs of their color — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — wins the match!

🧠 Pro Strategies to Win at Connect Four

  • Control the Center Column: The center column (Column 4) is part of the majority of potential 4-in-a-row winning combinations. Owning the center gives you massive positional leverage.
  • Set Up "7-Traps" and Double Threats: Create board states where you threaten two simultaneous 4-in-a-row connections on the same turn. Your opponent can only block one, guaranteeing your win on the next move!
  • Do Not Enable Your Opponent: Never drop a disc directly below a slot that would allow your opponent to complete 4 in a row on their next turn.
  • Look for Diagonal Traps: Diagonal 4-in-a-row paths are the hardest for opponents to spot. Build ascending diagonal threats early.
