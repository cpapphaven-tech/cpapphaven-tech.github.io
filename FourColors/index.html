🎮 How to Play Four Colors

  1. Initial Deal: Every player receives 7 cards. The remaining deck forms the Draw Pile, and the top card is flipped face-up to begin the Discard Pile.
  2. Matching Cards: On your turn, play a card that matches the active card by color, number, or action symbol.
  3. Playing Wild Cards: Wild cards can be played on any turn, allowing you to select which of the 4 colors the next player must play.
  4. Drawing from Deck: If you do not hold a valid matching card, tap the Draw Deck. If the newly drawn card is playable, you can lay it down immediately!
  5. Calling 1-CARD: When down to your last card, tap the glowing ⚡ 1-CARD button before your turn passes.
  6. Winning: The first player to shed all cards from their hand wins the round!

🧠 Master Strategies for Four Colors

  • Hold Wild Cards for the Endgame: Keep at least one Wild card in reserve so you never get trapped drawing cards when an opponent switches colors late in the game.
  • Anticipate Direction Changes: In 3 and 4-player matches, monitor which player holds the fewest cards and use Reverse or Skip cards to cut off their turns.
  • Watch Color Tendencies: Pay attention to which colors your opponents draw on. Forcing play into an opponent's weak color keeps their hand full!
