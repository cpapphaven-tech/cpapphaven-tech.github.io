How to Play Hangman

Hangman is the classic vocabulary and spelling game where your goal is to figure out the hidden word before running out of guesses.

Game Rules

  • Guess a Letter: Use the on-screen keyboard to guess a letter you think is in the hidden word.
  • Correct Guesses: The letter will be revealed in its correct position(s) in the blanks.
  • Incorrect Guesses: A new part of the hangman figure is drawn on the gallows.

You have exactly 6 mistakes allowed (Head, Body, Left Arm, Right Arm, Left Leg, Right Leg). If the drawing is completed before you reveal the word, it's Game Over! Can you build the ultimate winning streak?
