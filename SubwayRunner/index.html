🎮 How to Play Subway Runner 3D

The goal is straightforward: run as far down the railway lines as possible without crashing into oncoming subway trains, roadblocks, or overhead bridge signs while collecting shiny gold coins to multiply your score.

  1. Switch Lanes (Left & Right): Use ◀ Left Arrow / A and ▶ Right Arrow / D to dodge incoming trains across the 3 parallel railway tracks. On mobile, swipe left or right or use the touch buttons.
  2. Leap Over Roadblocks: Press ▲ Up Arrow / Space / W to jump clear over yellow-and-black hazard barriers and leap onto train cars.
  3. Roll / Duck Under Overhead Signs: Press ▼ Down Arrow / S to drop into a fast slide and glide safely underneath high overhead clearance signs.
  4. Collect Spinning Coins: Grab golden coins to boost your bank and climb the leaderboard.

  • Look Several Chunks Ahead: Never stare directly at your runner character. Focus your gaze 20–30 meters ahead down the tracks to prepare for upcoming train configurations.
