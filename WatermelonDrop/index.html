🎮 How to Play Watermelon Drop

  1. Aim the Cloud Dropper: Slide your finger across the top on mobile, or move your mouse on desktop. The dotted guide line projects where your fruit will land.
  2. Drop the Fruit: Release your touch or click the mouse to release the fruit into the glass container.
  3. Chain Merges: When two matching fruits collide, they pop and merge into the next tier. Chain reactions can clear space and generate huge combo score bonuses!
  4. Mind the Danger Line: If fruits stack up and cross the dashed red warning line for more than 3 continuous seconds, your container overflows and the game ends!
  5. Use the Nudge / Shake Button: Got an awkward jam where two matching fruits are separated by a millimeter? Use the 🫨 Nudge button to give the container a physical shake!

🧠 Top Pro Strategies & Tips

  • Keep Largest Fruits at the Bottom: Try to anchor your big melons, pineapples, and peaches near the floor and corners. If a huge melon sits on top of tiny cherries, it prevents the cherries from ever meeting their twins!
  • Build an Ascending Slope: Arrange your fruits in an escalating staircase from left to right (e.g., Cherry -> Strawberry -> Grape -> Orange -> Apple). When a new fruit is dropped, it can trigger a domino cascade of sequential merges!
  • Check the Next Fruit Indicator: Always look at the top-right "Next Fruit" preview box before choosing where to place your current fruit. Planning two steps ahead is the key to 3,000+ high scores.
  • Utilize Fruit Bounce Momentum: Dropping a fruit from high up gives it kinetic energy to roll down slopes and push smaller fruits out of the way.
