About Cyber Dino: Downhill Triple Jump Runner

Cyber Dino (also known as T-Rex Runner or the No Internet Dinosaur Game) is the most iconic Easter egg video game in internet history. Built into Google Chrome in 2014, the game features a pixelated Tyrannosaurus rex sprinting across a prehistoric desert landscape, leaping over sharp cacti and ducking under soaring pterodactyls.

🎮 Game Rules & Controls

  • Desktop Controls:
    • Spacebar / Up Arrow / W: Perform mid-air triple jumps, collect energy crystals, leap over downhill energy spikes and low birds.
    • Down Arrow / S: Duck under flying pterodactyls (and fast-drop while airborne).
  • Mobile Controls: Tap ▲ JUMP to leap and hold ▼ DUCK to crouch.
  • Dodge Obstacles: Colliding with any cactus or bird immediately ends the run.
  • Score Milestones: Hear a retro double-beep every 100 points as scrolling speed escalates.

🌟 Pro Tips to Score 10,000+ Points

  • Master the Fast-Fall: Tapping ▼ Down in mid-air pulls your dinosaur down immediately. This gives you instant control when an obstacle appears right after a jump.
  • High-Speed Jump Timing: At speeds above 1,000 points, wait until you are within 2 body lengths of a cactus before jumping to avoid overshooting and landing onto the hazard.
  • Memorize Bird Heights: High birds can be run under without crouching. Mid-height birds require ducking. Low birds require jumping.
