About Classic Four Colors Card Game

Four Colors (also known as Draughts in the UK and Europe, and Damas in Spanish-speaking regions) is an ancient, universally beloved two-player board game of pure diagonal tactics. Played on an 8x8 grid with 12 pieces per player, the goal is to capture all opposing pieces or trap your rival so they cannot make any legal moves.

🎮 Game Rules & Controls

  • The Objective: Race to empty all 7 starting cards from your hand before your opponents.
  • Color & Number Matching: Play any card that matches the active discard pile card by Color (Red, Green, Blue, Yellow) or Number/Symbol (0 to 9).
  • Action Cards:
    • 🚫 Skip: Bypasses the next player's turn.
    • 🔄 Reverse: Reverses turn direction (acts as Skip in 2-player games).
    • ➕2️⃣ Draw Two: Next player draws 2 cards and forfeits their turn.
    • 🌈 Wild: Play anytime to pick the next active color.
    • 💥 Wild Draw 4: Pick the next color AND make the next player draw 4 cards!
  • Draw Deck: If you have no playable cards, tap the draw deck. If the drawn card is playable, you can lay it down immediately!
  • ⚡ 1-CARD Shout: When down to your last card, tap the glowing 1-CARD button within 3 seconds or receive 2 penalty cards!

🌟 Master Strategies for Victory

  • Conserve Your Wilds: Save Wild cards for the late game. Having a Wild card as your final card or second-to-last card guarantees you can close out the match without getting stuck drawing.
  • Color Control & Forcing: Pay attention to when an opponent draws cards. If a bot draws when the color is Green, keep the table on Green as long as possible to force them to keep drawing!
  • Chain Action Pressure: If you hold a Skip and a Draw 2, play the Skip first to keep your turn initiative, then hit your opponent with Draw 2 right before they reach 1 card!
  • Never Forget 1-CARD: Watch your hand count closely! Missing the 1-CARD button call costs you 2 penalty cards and ruins your victory sprint.
