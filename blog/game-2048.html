About 2048 Number Puzzle

2048 is the legendary number puzzle game where players slide numbered tiles on a 4x4 grid. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one with double the value!

Whether you are playing on a desktop PC with precise keyboard and mouse controls or enjoying responsive touch gestures on your smartphone or tablet, 2048 Number Puzzle delivers an ultra-smooth, lightweight gaming experience with zero installation requirements.
