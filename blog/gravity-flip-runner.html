Mastering the Colour Switch

Gravity Flip Runner is a test of rhythm, patience, and colour recognition. One wrong tap ends the run.

Top 4 Pro Tips

1. Don't Spam Taps

Rhythmic single taps let you hover beneath an obstacle until the right gap opens.

2. Anticipate Colour Changes

Prepare for your ball colour to change near an orb.

3. Aim for Stars

Stars sit dead-centre of obstacles — clean colour-matched entry grabs them.

4. Use Soft Resting Taps

Inside large rings, tap gently to wait for the gap to rotate into position.
