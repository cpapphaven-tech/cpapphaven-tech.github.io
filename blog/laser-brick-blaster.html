Top 4 Pro Tips

1. Aim for Corners

Angling into corners clears large brick clusters quickly.

2. Use Paddle Angle

Ball reflection angle depends on where it hits the paddle — use this for precision.

3. Target Power-Up Bricks

Glowing bricks have power-ups — hit them early.

4. Stay Centred

Keep the paddle near the ball's X position rather than chasing it.
