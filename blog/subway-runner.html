🎮 Game Rules & Controls

  • Mobile Controls: Swipe in any direction on your touchscreen (left, right, up to jump, down to roll) or tap the dedicated on-screen touch bar (◀ LEFT, ▲ JUMP, ▼ ROLL, ▶ RIGHT).
