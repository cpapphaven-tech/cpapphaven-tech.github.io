How to Play 2048

Slide tiles on the 4×4 grid to merge matching numbers:

  • Desktop: Use arrow keys (↑ ↓ ← →) to slide all tiles.
  • Mobile: Swipe Up, Down, Left, or Right on the board.
  • Goal: Merge tiles with the same number — they add together. Reach the 2048 tile to win!
  • Game Over: If the board fills up and no moves are left, the game ends.
  • Tip: Keep your highest tile in a corner for maximum control.

About 2048

2048 is one of the most popular puzzle games ever created. Originally made by Gabriele Cirulli in 2014, it became a global sensation — played by hundreds of millions of people worldwide. The concept is deceptively simple: merge tiles to double them, and keep going until you hit 2048 (or beyond!).
