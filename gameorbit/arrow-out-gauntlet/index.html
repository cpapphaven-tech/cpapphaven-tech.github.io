How to Play Arrow Out Gauntlet

Arrow Out Gauntlet packs multiple moving targets into 20 stages! Limited arrows mean every shot counts. Hit the bullseye zone for 3x points. Can you clear all 20?

Controls

Tap or click on the screen to interact with the game elements.

Objective

Score as many points as possible before time runs out or you lose all lives.

Game Over

The game ends when the timer reaches zero or you fail the level conditions.
