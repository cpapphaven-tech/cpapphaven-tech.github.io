🥚 About Egg Sort: Easter Color Puzzle

Egg Sort is a relaxing yet strategic color puzzle game. Drop beautifully decorated Easter eggs from trays above, watch them land on the ever-moving circular conveyor belt, and guide them into the matching color nests below. With only 3 slots per nest, every drop counts!

As levels progress, more colors and trays are introduced, making the puzzle increasingly tricky. Calm ASMR-style physics and smooth egg animations make this the perfect stress-relief game.

🎮 How to Play

  • 🥚 Tap a tray at the top to drop an egg onto the conveyor
  • 🔄 Conveyor keeps moving – plan ahead before dropping!
  • 📦 Auto-Pack: Eggs will automatically drop into matching color nests as they pass underneath
  • ✅ Complete nests – fill all 3 slots with the same color
  • ⚠️ Don't overfill – if belt is full with no matches, you lose!

🏆 Tips & Tricks

  • 📌 Check which nests are partially filled before dropping eggs
  • 🧠 Think 2-3 moves ahead – the belt moves constantly
  • ⚡ Drop eggs strategically to avoid overflowing the belt
  • 🎯 Try to complete a nest efficiently to earn bonus points
