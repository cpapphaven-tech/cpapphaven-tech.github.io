Gem Match – Free Online Puzzle Game

Play free online Gem Match game. Swap adjacent gems to form vertical or horizontal lines of three or more matching gems. Every match boosts your score and drops new gems from above! Challenge yourself to reach higher levels and face bigger targets.

How to Play

Tap and drag a gem towards an adjacent gem to swap them. If the swap creates a match of 3 or more identical gems, they will pop, and you'll earn points. If the swap doesn't result in a match, the gems will return to their original positions.
