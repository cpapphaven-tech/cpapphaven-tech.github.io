About Geometry Jump

Dash and jump through a fast-paced obstacle course! Geometry Jump is a rhythm-action platformer game inspired by Geometry Dash. Control your neon square as it speeds forward, and time your jumps perfectly to leap over sharp spikes, drop through gaps, and slide onto elevated platforms.

Unlock new levels, challenge your reflexes, and see how far you can make it before crashing. Complete 100% of the course to clear the level and progress!

How to Play

  • Tap/Click: Jump.
  • Hold Tap/Click: Continuous jumps.
  • Objective: Reach the end of the track (100% progress) without hitting any spikes or blocks.
