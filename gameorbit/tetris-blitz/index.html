How to Play Tetris

Tetris is a timeless puzzle game where you must strategically place falling blocks, known as Tetrominoes. The goal is to create complete horizontal lines of blocks without any gaps. When a line is complete, it disappears, granting you points and freeing up space.

Game Rules & Tips

  • Clear Lines: Try to clear multiple lines at once (a "Tetris") for maximum points.
  • Plan Ahead: Always keep an eye on the "Next" box to see which piece is coming up.
  • Keep it Flat: Avoid creating deep wells or tall spires. Keep your stack as flat as possible.
  • Level Up: As you clear lines, the level increases, causing the blocks to fall faster!
