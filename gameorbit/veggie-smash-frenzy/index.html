Veggie Smash – Free Online Puzzle Game

Play free online Veggie Smash game. Swap adjacent veggies to form vertical or horizontal lines of three or more matching veggies. Every match boosts your score and drops new veggies from above! Challenge yourself to reach higher levels and face bigger targets.

How to Play

Tap and drag a veggie towards an adjacent veggie to swap them. If the swap creates a match of 3 or more identical veggies, they will pop, and you'll earn points. If the swap doesn't result in a match, the veggies will return to their original positions.
